# Access layers — owner decision, 25 Nov 2026
Draft only. No production changes.
Public material: only approved institutional content and consumer-facing experience descriptions, never the full commercial catalog. Public contact capture is cross-destination, not a booking. Suggested paths: / for public site, /interes for form, asociados.hotelexperience.org/catalogo/atacama for authenticated commercial material; exact URLs subject to confirmation. Existing public /catalogo/atacama must be reviewed and sanitized or moved behind auth before public promotion; do not assume current copy safe.

Public form: name and contact (email or phone), destination, optional approximate dates, pax and interest, privacy notice and consent. No price, discount, partner agreement, seller routing, payment, ID/passport, health notes, full passenger list, reservation or confirmed service. On submit return opaque receipt, no ability to retrieve or edit arbitrary leads by UUID. Challenge, rate limit, server validation and idempotency; no raw table grants. Customers do not need login for interest, and cannot self-register as associates. Keep prior candidate intake SQL blocked pending minimal public contract rewrite.

Authenticated associate: invitation/approved account only. Reuse existing partner_portal_accounts and partner_portal_sessions after auditing authentication, expiry, revocation, scope and server APIs; do not create parallel identity store by default. Bind partner/hotel ID and permissions to verified session on server, not client input. Scope catalog prices, documents, requests and reservation records to the agreement and destination. Full form uses existing lead when authorized; associate cannot claim an arbitrary web lead by knowing a receipt/UUID. Authorized manager/seller links or assigns public lead to partner. Associate-created reservation can use a new lead with trusted origin. No duplicate lead on handoff. Internal supplier cost, margins, unrelated partners and internal finance not shown to associates unless explicit need and permission.

Sales: existing active profiles and assigned seller scope; no public role provisioning. Confirm and collect according to agreement. Opera: admin/manager or authorized operational role per scope, not partner or seller by default. All authorization enforced server-side/RLS including direct RPC and file downloads; deny-by-default, private/no-store, no public indexed commercial documents, no embedded secrets. Review legacy workers.dev URL, old PDF paths, previews, APIs and public Supabase functions; new host restrictions alone do not close old access. get_public_products returns public-facing fields including origin; public_product_options returns names/category/origin, not prices in checked signatures. Review actual content and legacy callers before changing grants. Do not blindly disable current production flows.
 
Acceptance: anonymous can submit interest only; cannot open full catalog/form, get price sheets or fetch PII; direct calls also denied. Inactive/revoked account denied. Associate A cannot see or submit for B even with known IDs. Sales agent only authorized lead; no operator-only data. Repeated intake/booking submits produce same identity. Authenticate, verify scope, continue existing lead and apply agreement; customer payment and HE settlement separately tracked. Test protected paths on old and new host, cached assets and API, then release. No production migrations or real bookings until staging passes.
